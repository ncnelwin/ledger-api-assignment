Assumptions
• All monetary values are stored in cents to avoid rounding issues, with a maximum of two
decimal places.
• New accounts start with a zero balance and receive a unique ID.
• Debits are allowed only when sufficient funds are available.
• Transfers must succeed completely or have no effect, ensuring balances remain consistent.
• Transactions cannot be modified once recorded.
• Pagination is supported, with a default limit of 10 and a maximum of 100 records per request.
• SQLite is used as the database, with transactions to maintain data consistency.
• Authentication is outside the scope of this assignment.
